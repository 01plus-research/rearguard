RearGuard: can it be built?RearGuard：能做出来吗？

Feasibility study · rear-approach radar warning for cyclists · 10th grade, 16 weeks × 4 h, $300 · researched 13 Oct 2026可行性研究 · 骑行后方来车雷达预警 · 10 年级，16 周 × 每周 4 小时，预算 $300 · 调研日期 2026 年 10 月 13 日

Feasible with changes调整后可行

The sensing is solved off the shelf: the Hi-Link HLK-LD2451 is a $6–10, 24 GHz radar module built for rear-vehicle detection that already reports each target's distance, angle and speed, and maintained Arduino libraries read it. The student's real work is the rule that tells a car from a passing cyclist, and proving it with labelled rides. That fits 16 weeks only if the phone app is dropped from the first version, nothing is designed from scratch, and the first tests happen off public roads.

感知部分已有现成方案：Hi-Link HLK-LD2451 是一块 $6–10 的 24 GHz 雷达模块，本来就是为后方来车检测设计的，能直接输出每个目标的距离、角度和速度，并且有仍在维护的 Arduino 库。学生真正要做的，是写出区分"汽车"和"超车的骑行者"的判断规则，并用带标注的骑行数据证明它有效。要在 16 周内完成，前提是：第一版不做手机 App，不从零设计任何电路，最初的测试不上公共道路。

1. The MVP1. 最小可行产品（MVP）

A rear unit on the seatpost (LD2451 radar + ESP32 board + USB power bank) logs every radar frame and sends an alert over ESP-NOW to a handlebar unit (ESP32 board + haptic driver + vibration motor + buzzer). A car closing from behind gives a strong double buzz. A cyclist overtaking gives nothing, or a single short pulse.

座管上的后部单元（LD2451 雷达 + ESP32 开发板 + USB 充电宝）记录每一帧雷达数据，并通过 ESP-NOW 向车把单元（ESP32 开发板 + 触觉驱动板 + 振动马达 + 蜂鸣器）发送警报。后方有汽车接近时，车把强烈振动两下；骑行者超车时不振动，或只短振一下。

The deliverable is a number: car-detection rate and cyclist false-alert rate, measured against video of real rides, compared with a Garmin Varia RTL515 on the same rides.

最终成果是一组数字：以真实骑行的视频为标准，测出汽车检出率和骑行者误报率，并与同一次骑行中 Garmin Varia RTL515 的表现对比。

Two-minute demo两分钟演示

Play a 30-second split-screen clip: rear video, live radar trace, and the moment the handlebar buzzes as a car approaches and stays quiet as a cyclist passes. Then show the confusion table from the logged rides. Finally, hand the judge the handlebar unit and trigger it.

播放 30 秒分屏视频：后方画面、实时雷达轨迹，以及汽车接近时车把振动、骑行者超车时保持安静的瞬间。然后展示记录骑行得出的混淆矩阵。最后把车把单元交给评委，现场触发一次。

Out of scope for version 1第一版不做

  • Phone app. Analysis is done in Python from the CSV log; a BLE phone display is a stretch goal only.
  • 手机 App。用 Python 分析 CSV 日志；手机蓝牙显示只作为加分项。
  • Custom PCB, machine learning, a helmet unit, a tail light, and waterproofing beyond a rain cover.
  • 定制 PCB、机器学习、头盔版本、尾灯，以及超出防雨罩程度的防水。

2. What already exists2. 已有的产品与项目

  • Commercial radars. Garmin Varia RTL515 (~$150, 140 m range), RearVue 820, Bryton, Lezyne and Cycplus all warn of approaching vehicles. Reviews report misses and false positives. The RTL515 only reports that "something faster than you is closing", without classifying it.
  • 商用雷达。Garmin Varia RTL515（约 $150，探测距离 140 m）、RearVue 820、Bryton、Lezyne、Cycplus 都能提示后方来车。评测提到它们有漏报和误报。RTL515 只报告"有比你快的东西在接近"，不判断是什么。
  • The module. Hi-Link sells the LD2451 for rear-vehicle detection on non-motorised lanes and motorcycles. Libraries: HLK-LD245X (Arduino, BSD-3, updated May 2026) and esphome-ld2451 (updated Sep 2026).
  • 雷达模块。Hi-Link 将 LD2451 定位为非机动车道和摩托车的后方来车检测模块。可用的库：HLK-LD245X（Arduino，BSD-3，2026 年 5 月仍有更新）和 esphome-ld2451（2026 年 9 月更新）。
  • DIY. No published LD2451 bike radar was found. OpenELAB's Radar Cube is a hobby rear-warning device for bikes. No peer-reviewed accuracy study of bike radars was found.
  • DIY 项目。没有找到已发布的 LD2451 自行车雷达项目。OpenELAB 的 Radar Cube 是一个业余的自行车后方预警装置。也没有找到经过同行评审的自行车雷达准确率研究。

What is still the student's own: an open, measured car-vs-cyclist rule and a labelled ride dataset with honest error rates, benchmarked against a commercial unit.

学生自己的贡献：一套公开、经过测量的"汽车 vs 骑行者"判断规则，加上一份带标注、如实报告误差的骑行数据集，并与商用产品做对比。

3. The hard part3. 真正的难点

The LD2451 outputs, per target (up to 5): distance, horizontal angle and radial speed relative to the sensor. It can filter by direction and by minimum speed (0–120 km/h). It gives no target size or type. So the classifier is mostly closing speed: riding at 20 km/h, a cyclist at 28 km/h closes at 8 km/h, while a car at 50 km/h closes at 30 km/h.

LD2451 对每个目标（最多 5 个）输出：距离、水平角度、相对传感器的径向速度。它可以按方向和最低速度（0–120 km/h）过滤，但不输出目标大小或类型。所以判断主要依靠接近速度：自己以 20 km/h 骑行时，28 km/h 的骑行者接近速度是 8 km/h，50 km/h 的汽车是 30 km/h。

That separates the common case. It fails where speeds overlap: fast e-bikes, cars crawling in traffic, and the rider stopped at a light. Whether distance, angle or track persistence closes the gap is unknown until tested. The test is roughly 150 labelled overtakes. Also unknown: how well the module, which is sold for fixed and vehicle mounting, copes with a vibrating seatpost. Bench and parking-lot tests in weeks 4–8 settle that before any road riding.

这能区分大多数情况，但在速度重叠时会失效：快速电助力车、堵车中缓行的汽车、骑行者在红灯前停车。距离、角度或轨迹持续时间能否弥补这一点，要测了才知道，大约需要 150 次带标注的超车。另一个未知数：这块模块是为固定安装和机动车设计的，装在振动的座管上表现如何？第 4–8 周的台架和停车场测试会在上路之前回答这个问题。

8. If it goes wrong, then what8. 出问题时怎么办

  1. Cut first: the Varia comparison, then the buzzer, then the second validation ride.
  2. 先砍：Varia 对比，然后是蜂鸣器，再然后是第二次验证骑行。
  3. If on-road data is blocked (approval or safety), run the whole study in the lot with controlled passes at known speeds. That is still a valid engineering test.
  4. 如果路测受阻（审批或安全原因），全部研究改在停车场以已知速度做受控通过测试。这仍然是有效的工程测试。
  5. If the car/cyclist rule fails, the project becomes "a $54 open rear radar, and where speed-only classification breaks". That is an honest negative result with data.
  6. 如果"汽车/骑行者"规则失败，项目就变成"一台 $54 的开源后视雷达，以及纯速度分类在哪些情况下失效"。这是有数据支撑的、诚实的负面结果。

9. Where it could go9. 后续方向

A solid regional science-fair entry (ISEF category: Embedded Systems or Engineering Technology), with the dataset and error rates as the strength. A student-journal paper (e.g. Journal of Emerging Investigators) is realistic only with 150+ labelled overtakes and a clear comparison to the Varia. No patent case: commercial radars already exist. Version 2 could add the BLE phone app and a helmet unit.

适合作为地区科学展的扎实参赛项目（ISEF 类别：嵌入式系统或工程技术），数据集和误差率是亮点。只有拿到 150 次以上带标注的超车数据并与 Varia 清晰对比，才有可能投学生期刊（如 Journal of Emerging Investigators）。不适合申请专利，因为商用雷达已经存在。第二版可以加入蓝牙手机 App 和头盔单元。
